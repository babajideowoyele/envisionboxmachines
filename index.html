The sphere with nothing to hide

A machine has no identity to protect, and neither does a rendered model. That makes this the only sphere where a method can be measured with nothing removed, which is what the other three get compared against.

Where this sphere stands

Counted from the sphere matrix, not estimated. A method can carry a partial finding and an open question at once, so the two numbers below are not meant to add up to the first.

What the trade is here

The identifying signal and the behavioural signal are the same pixels. What differs between spheres is how far they overlap.

What identifies a subject

None for a machine or a rendered model. Any human interacting with it carries the full human case.

What the law requires

None for the machine or the model.

Why it is hard

There is no trade to make, which is what makes this sphere useful: it is the only one where a method can be measured with nothing hidden.
